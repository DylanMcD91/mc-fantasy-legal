Privacy Policy for Royal Fantasy

Last updated: 07/20/2026

Royal Fantasy ("the App") is operated by Royal Blazers LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using Royal Fantasy, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you create an account, we collect a username and email address (both required), and optionally your first and last name. We also collect a password, which is stored only in a securely hashed form — we never store your password in plain text.

Information you generate by using the App. As you use Royal Fantasy, we store data associated with your account, including your fantasy teams and rosters, the leagues you join or create, your in-app budget and transaction history, wagers you propose or accept, weekly awards, chat messages you post in league chats, and your selected profile avatar. This information is necessary for the App to function.

Technical and error data. We use Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service, to help us detect and fix crashes and bugs. When an error occurs, Sentry may collect technical information such as device type, operating system version, and a diagnostic error report. We have configured Sentry to exclude sensitive information such as passwords, authentication tokens, and personal account details from these reports.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: create and maintain your account; operate the App's features, including teams, leagues, the player economy, wagers, and awards; display your profile and activity to other members of leagues you belong to; diagnose and fix technical problems; and, if you use a future password-recovery feature, send you a password-reset email. Email addresses are used only for account identification and, in the future, password recovery. We do not send marketing or promotional email.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Within the App. Certain information — such as your username, avatar, team, and activity within a league — is visible to other members of leagues you join. Chat messages you post are visible to other members of that league.

Service providers. We share information with third-party services that make the App work: Render, which provides our hosting and database infrastructure, and Sentry, which provides error monitoring. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound to protect it.

Third-party sports data. The App displays NFL player information, statistics, and images sourced from the Sleeper API, and NFL scores sourced from the ESPN API. This is publicly available sports data displayed within the App; these sources do not receive your personal information.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the App, under Account Settings. When you delete your account, we permanently remove your personal information and the data associated with your teams. Certain records that involve other users — such as a wager's outcome in another user's history, or messages within a shared league — may be retained in anonymized form so that other users' records remain intact, but these will no longer be associated with your identity.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including hashing passwords and transmitting data over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Royal Fantasy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
